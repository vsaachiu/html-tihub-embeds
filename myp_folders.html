Secondary Transition

Launching the ✶MYP Folder System with Year 6

Transitioning to Year 6 and the MYP is an exciting milestone. Students go from managing one primary classroom to balancing multiple subjects, specialist teachers, and digital workflows. Use these routines to make the shift smooth, empowering, and fun!

1

The Week 1 "Star & Bookmark" Session (5 Minutes)

Dedicate the first five minutes of homeroom or your initial subject class to having students open their *MYP folder using the direct link and immediately press Ctrl+D (or Cmd+D) to bookmark it on their browser bar. Instant, one-click access all year long!

2

Model the "Save-It-Right" Habit

When kicking off a new task, project, or investigation, model navigating into the designated subject subfolder (e.g., Science - MYP1) before clicking New → Google Docs / Slides. This eliminates the common primary habit of files floating around in the root of "My Drive."

3

Celebrate ATL Self-Management Wins

Frame digital organization as an authentic IB Approaches to Learning (ATL) self-management skill. A quick verbal shout-out or homeroom recognition for students whose digital workspace is organized reinforces positive digital citizenship throughout Year 6.

Pro-Tip for Year 6 Homeroom Advisors

Schedule a 10-minute "Digital Desk Cleanup" on the last Friday of each month. Have students review their Google Drive root, sort loose documents into their respective *MYP subject subfolders, and verify that shared slides or notes are properly filed.

Technical Framework

System Architecture & Access Model

The folder hierarchy synchronises directly with ManageBac timetable data. Permissions are granted with silent notifications to ensure clean communication.

Google Drive Hierarchy Auto-Maintained
#MYP ALL *MYP STUDENTS Top-Level Root • All Teachers = Editor
└── Year <cohort> (e.g., Year 6 / Year 7)
└── <homeroom> (e.g., 6V, 6S, 7A)
└── *MYP <Full Student Name> - <Student ID> Student = Editor • Parents = Viewer
├── Language and Literature - <Class Code>
├── Sciences - <Class Code>
└── Design / Mathematics / Arts ...
User Role Permission Level Scope & System Behavior
MYP Teachers Editor Inherited from the top-level #MYP ALL *MYP STUDENTS directory. Grants instant edit and comment access across all cohorts, homerooms, and subject folders.
Students Editor Applied directly to their personal *MYP <Student Name> folder and all nested subject subfolders.
Parents / Guardians Viewer Applied silently as read-only viewers on their child's *MYP folder. Protects student work integrity while providing complete transparency.

*Note: All permissions are provisioned silently via Google Drive API to prevent notification spam across student and parent email accounts.

Strategic Value

Why We Love the ✶MYP Folder System

Built to reduce administrative barriers, enhance classroom feedback, and build transparent learning partnerships across Victoria Shanghai Academy.

Frictionless Access & Zero Roadblocks

  • No "Request Access" emails: Teachers have open Editor rights to provide timely, live formative feedback.
  • Automated Term Setup: ManageBac synchronization builds and updates class folders automatically.
  • Zero Email Clutter: Background API provisioning keeps student and family inboxes pristine.

Consistency & ATL Self-Management

  • Uniform School Structure: The digital experience is identical whether in Science, Math, or Visual Arts.
  • No Lost Work: Eliminate "I lost my file" moments; drafts live within dedicated subject containers.
  • Self-Regulated Learning: Reinforces authentic IB Approaches to Learning (ATL) organizational habits.

Pastoral & Cross-Department Visibility

  • Homeroom Holistic Insight: Advisors can review a student's full academic output and overall workload.
  • Inclusive Learning Support: EAL, Learning Support, and counselors have immediate access to all subjects.
  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Teachers can inspect work across departments to coordinate assessments.

Transparent Home-School Partnership

  • Real-Time Window into Learning: Parents view active drafts, design folios, and projects continuously.
  • Effortless Student-Led Conferences: A student's entire portfolio is neatly cataloged and ready to share.
  • Safe Read-Only Guardrails: Protects against accidental deletion while maintaining complete transparency.
